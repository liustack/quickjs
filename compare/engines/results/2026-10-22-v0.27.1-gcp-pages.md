### What the polyfill costs liustack-quickjs

- Loading it: 384 ms per VM, before the bundle (411 ms after it, 305 ms without it).
- Memory per VM, polyfill: liustack-quickjs 34.5 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 107.5 MiB RSS (80.9 MiB Go heap)
- Memory per VM, own Intl: liustack-quickjs 12.6 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 51.1 MiB RSS (41.0 MiB Go heap)
- Time inside the polyfill: 214.7 ms of 928.3 ms per render summed over the pages (23%), 1354 calls.
- Time inside its own Intl: 5.6 ms of 697.3 ms per render summed over the pages (1%), 1354 calls.
