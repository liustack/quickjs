### What the polyfill costs liustack-quickjs

- Loading it: 385 ms per VM, before the bundle (295 ms after it, 305 ms without it).
- Memory per VM, polyfill: liustack-quickjs 34.7 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 108.3 MiB RSS (82.0 MiB Go heap)
- Memory per VM, own Intl: liustack-quickjs 12.6 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 50.3 MiB RSS (41.4 MiB Go heap)
- Time inside the polyfill: 224.3 ms of 990.9 ms per render summed over the pages (23%), 1354 calls.
- Time inside its own Intl: 8.9 ms of 763.8 ms per render summed over the pages (1%), 1354 calls.
