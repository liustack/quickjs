### What the polyfill costs liustack-quickjs

- Loading it: 396 ms per VM, before the bundle (294 ms after it, 312 ms without it).
- Memory per VM, polyfill: liustack-quickjs 34.6 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 112.1 MiB RSS (81.5 MiB Go heap)
- Memory per VM, own Intl: liustack-quickjs 12.7 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 47.6 MiB RSS (39.8 MiB Go heap)
- Time inside the polyfill: 234.1 ms of 979.7 ms per render summed over the pages (24%), 1354 calls.
- Time inside its own Intl: 5.6 ms of 732.9 ms per render summed over the pages (1%), 1354 calls.
