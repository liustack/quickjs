### What the polyfill costs liustack-quickjs

- Loading it: 371 ms per VM, before the bundle (284 ms after it, 302 ms without it).
- Memory per VM, polyfill: liustack-quickjs 34.7 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 116.1 MiB RSS (82.6 MiB Go heap)
- Memory per VM, own Intl: liustack-quickjs 12.8 MiB RSS, go-quickjs-interp 48.0 MiB RSS (40.4 MiB Go heap)
- Time inside the polyfill: 217.1 ms of 939.2 ms per render summed over the pages (23%), 1354 calls.
- Time inside its own Intl: 5.6 ms of 710.3 ms per render summed over the pages (1%), 1354 calls.
